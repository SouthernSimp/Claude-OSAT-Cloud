# The rework: write it down, OSAT puts it where it goes (October 15, 2026)

Mockup to click through: https://claude.ai/artifact/2i4zwb7q15fKeyuCan9D48 (five screens, they work).

## What Nate said

The canvas "does not look visually easy to understand". Every line leaves one point on the topic, the branches are
small pills in one column, most of the board is empty and nothing says what matters. The real problem is intake:
some days a few stickies, some days fifty, and sorting them is the chore. He wants to drop stickies in one at a time
(or a photo of paper ones) and never worry again: questions go to a questions place, a grocery list goes to the
grocery list, a date goes to his real calendar, meetings come in from Granola. The AI decides the details. It should
feel like n8n (seeing data move through the wires) and like Muse (one chat, a SOUL file and a memory you can read),
and it should become the one-stop shop a small business owner gets on a Mac mini: online if they want, offline if
they don't.

## What OSAT already has, and what was wrong with it

Most of the pieces exist already: the AI files stickies on its own (Phase 35), scans and photos are read by Vision
(Phase 15), the Mac's Calendar (Phase 14), Meetings (57), overnight ideas (53), the morning brief (49), bots, cloud
models, the MCP client (48a) and webhooks (46). They fail for three reasons:

1. **You can't see them work.** Filing happens silently, so you never trust it, so you sort by hand anyway.
2. **There are six ways to sort.** Sort a pile, Sort Unsorted, Help me sort, Suggest homes, Put the desk away and
   auto-filing each have their own screen and their own words.
3. **The canvas has five kinds of thing**: topics, branches, loose branches, free stickies and stacks, plus lines
   that can mean anything. The screenshot is what "Put the desk away" draws from that.

## The decisions

**Boards.** A board is an area of your life or work: OSAT, Fortis, mccreery.ai, Home, Personal. A new user starts
with Personal, Home and Work. When three or more stickies share a subject no board has, OSAT offers a new board
(the overnight "topic" idea, kept). Boards replace topics, branches, loose branches and stacks.

**Every sticky gets a kind**: Idea, Question, Problem, To-do, Date, To buy or Note. The kind does the organizing.
The kind and the board are separate questions: what is it, and what is it about.

**Lists OSAT keeps for you**: Questions (every open question from every board), Groceries and To buy, Coming up.
A Date goes to the Mac's own Calendar, which already passes it on to Google or Outlook when that account is added in
System Settings, so no Google sign-in is needed for v1.

**One board, three views**: Map (the canvas: one column per kind, so nothing tangles), Board (kanban: Later, Next,
Doing, Done) and List. Lines on the Map mean only "related" and are faint. Hierarchy comes from columns, not lines.

**Just in** replaces Unsorted and every sorting screen: what came in, where it went, Undo on each, a photo becomes one
row per sticky, and when OSAT isn't sure it asks with two buttons. The morning brief sits on top.

**Flows**, n8n-style, run on the Mac. The intake itself is the first flow: a sticky comes in → Read it (AI on this
Mac) → What is it? → Calendar / Groceries / Questions / its board. Each step lights up as a sticky passes through,
counts what went through today, and opens a panel with its settings and the last few stickies. More flows come later:
Meeting → to-dos, Morning brief, then flows you build from When… / AI / Do / Ask me steps.

**Soul and memory**: three plain files OSAT reads first. SOUL.md (who it is, how it talks), MEMORY.md (what it learned,
each line with where it learned it, Forget on each) and RULES.md (On its own / Ask me / Never for filing, lists, the
calendar, new boards, sending anything off the Mac, spending money). "About you" (Phase 17) moves into MEMORY.md.

**Connections**: one screen of tiles. The AI (built in, Claude, ChatGPT, any key, an MCP tool server) and accounts
(this Mac, Google, Microsoft 365, Granola, Slack, Notion, GitHub, the iPhone). One Online/Offline switch turns them
all off at once. Granola goes through its MCP server with the client OSAT already has.

**No notepad.** The capture line is the notepad: anything typed becomes a sticky and is filed.

## The phases

| Phase | What |
|---|---|
| 67 | What it is: every sticky gets a kind (code first, the AI in the filing run for the unclear ones), Lists OSAT keeps for you, dates to the calendar with Undo, Just in replaces Unsorted and Filed for you; the old boards stop being laid out on every load |
| 68 | Boards and the new canvas: topics become boards; Map / Board / List per board; the old Sky, its layouts and the sorting screens go |
| 69 | Flows: the intake as a flow you can watch, step panels, run history; then Meeting → to-dos and Morning brief |
| 70 | Soul and memory: SOUL.md, MEMORY.md, RULES.md, and every AI action checked against RULES.md |
| 71 | Connections: one screen, Granola through MCP, Slack and Notion, the Online/Offline switch |

Old data is kept: each topic becomes a board, each branch's stickies keep their words and get a kind. Nothing is
deleted; "Re-sort everything" is offered once, with Undo.

## What was under-built

- Nothing showed what the AI did. Just in and the Flows screen fix that.
- A photo of many paper stickies came in as one node; it should be one sticky each, filed one by one.
- Dates in stickies were noticed (Phase 28 idea) but never reached a calendar on their own.
- No single list of open questions, which is the "safety blanket".
- The AI's rules (what it may do alone) were scattered across Settings switches.
