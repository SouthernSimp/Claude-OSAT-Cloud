# What's new in OSAT

Every time work is merged, GitHub builds a new version of OSAT. Settings → About shows which one you have (0.1.280 is
build 280), and Check for updates installs the newest. Newest first. Each line ends with the pull request it came from.
OSAT shows this page in Tools → Roadmap → What’s new.

## Next build · October 24, 2026 — Ready for your client meetings, and Activity

- **Before a client meeting:** when today's calendar has a meeting whose name names one of your clients ("Fortis
  kickoff"), Today puts a card on top until it's over: when you last met (click to open that meeting), what's still
  open with them (tick it off right there, with Undo) and where they came up lately. Open the client's board from
  the card. (#128)
- **Been a while:** Today lists a client you've met before but not in three weeks, with when you last met, so no
  one slips. Click one to open their board. (#128)
- **Activity** (Power tools → Activity): how your Mac is doing without opening Activity Monitor. Memory used and
  whether there's room to spare, how busy the processor is, free disk space, the apps using the most memory with
  Quit (then Force quit if one won't go), and your Mac's specs. (#129)
- Fix: Kill Process and Uninstall in the quick bar couldn't list running apps on the Mac. (#129)

## Build 368 · October 22, 2026 — Bills & subscriptions, Money redone, 18 apps to sign in to, Today tidied

- **Bills & subscriptions:** Money finds what charges you again and again (Netflix, the phone bill, the gym) and
  lists it with your own plans, soonest first, with about what they come to a month. Not a bill puts one away, with
  Undo. Today names the bill due this week. (#125)
- **Money, redone:** one page that uses the whole window: the month at a glance (what's left over, money in and
  out), spending by category and cash flow in the middle, your accounts and bills beside them, and every entry
  grouped by day like your bank's own list. ‹ › steps through the months. With nothing in yet, one clear start:
  connect your bank, or add an entry by hand. (#126)
- **18 apps, one click each:** Connect an app now lists Notion, Airtable, monday.com, ClickUp, Todoist, Linear,
  Jira and Confluence, Calendly, Stripe, Square, PayPal, Canva, Figma, Intercom, Webflow, Wix, Zapier and Granola.
  Connect, sign in in your browser, done; Find an app narrows the list. (#124)
- **Today, tidied:** one centred page that sits in the middle of a wide window instead of hugging the left, with your
  boards, then to-dos beside your day, goals, habits and money. The row of counts and the Mail / Meetings / Files
  buttons are gone (the sidebar already has both), and Recent work no longer repeats your to-dos. (#127)

## Build 356 · October 22, 2026 — Canvases are back, Connect Gmail, your bank in Money, a clearer view of your work, clients, and to-dos from Granola and email

- **Canvases:** whiteboards in the sidebar, as many as you like. Stickies (the Sticky button or N), shapes, text
  boxes and arrows that hold on, for mind maps, and a live kanban of any board right on the canvas. Everything saves
  by itself and works offline. (#121)
- **The Board tab is called Kanban,** so it's easy to find: Later, Next, Doing, Done. (#121)
- **Connect Gmail:** Mail signs in to Gmail directly with Google's own sign-in, instead of going through the Mac's
  Mail app. Your inbox, Gmail's search and To-dos from email, read only: nothing is sent or changed. (#122)
- **Money, fully built out:** connect your bank through Plaid (your keys stay in the Mac's Keychain) and your
  accounts, balances and spending come in by themselves every hour. Spending by category as a donut and a table,
  a budget for any category, six months of cash flow, find any entry, and move one to another category with Undo.
  Transfers between your own accounts are never counted as spending. (#123)

- **Today brings your work together:** your boards with their real progress, recently edited notes, your day at a
  glance and shortcuts to Mail, Meetings and Files. Open a board or note straight from Today. Calendar entries
  open the calendar; dated stickies open their note. To-dos still tick off with Undo; habits, goals and money stay. (#116)
- **Every Granola meeting gives its to-dos:** notes written without a Next steps list are now read by the AI on
  this Mac, so the things promised still come into Just in. If the AI is asleep, OSAT tries again within the hour. (#118)
- **Clients:** right-click a board → Mark as a client. It moves to Clients in the sidebar, says when you last met,
  and its Overview lists every meeting with them and every note that names them. (#119)
- **Links read as words on cards:** a Granola to-do says "From Granola: Fortis kickoff", not a web address. (#119)
- **To-dos from email:** turn it on in Mail and, once an hour, what new mail asks of you comes into Just in as
  to-dos, saying which email. Only the AI on your Mac reads it; newsletters and robots are skipped; nothing is sent
  or changed in Mail; Take them back puts a batch in the Bin. (#120)

## Build 338 · October 21, 2026 — Ask knows the new OSAT, board overviews, roomier Notes, clearer Connections

- **Ask uses today's words:** boards and Just in, with directions for the sidebar and meeting follow-ups. It no
  longer sends you to the old Sky or sorting screens. (#112)
- **Ask sees your open to-dos,** in the same order as Today, with their board names. Today's journal checklist is
  named separately, so the two don't get mixed up. Your note-sharing choices still apply. (#112)
- **Notes opens with room to write:** the extra board column stays tucked away. Click the list's title (All notes,
  Questions, or a board's name) to browse lists, boards and tags. Pick a list, press Esc, or close Browse notes to
  return to writing. Your saved list, notes and boards stay as they are. (#113)
- **Overview on every board:** next steps, upcoming dates and recent notes together, with progress from the steps
  actually done. Tick a step off with Undo, open a note to read it, or switch to Map, Board or List for the details.
  A fresh start uses Overview; your saved view stays yours. Nothing is moved or rewritten. (#114)
- **Connections starts with your apps:** names, on/off switches and one Connect an app button. Open an app for
  sign-in, connection checks, what it can do and Remove with Undo. Sharing with other apps and automations wait
  behind their own rows. AI provider setup is now in AI, with keys only shown when connecting a provider. (#115)

## Build 321 · October 17, 2026 — The new OSAT: one window, Today, Goals and meeting to-dos

- **One window, one sidebar,** the way Apple's own apps work: Today, Just in, Calendar and To do at the top, your
  boards under them, then Business (Meetings, Mail), Life (Habits, Money, Journal), Library (Notes, Files) and the
  power tools (Web, Terminal, GitHub, Passwords, the Roadmap), folded until you open them. Everything opens in the same
  window; the desk, the dock and the floating windows are gone. ⌘1–6 open the first six places, and the window opens
  where you left it. (#108)
- **Today** is the first thing you see: a greeting and the morning brief (or one line that sums up the day), what’s
  to do (tick it off, with Undo), what waits in Just in, today’s calendar and what’s coming up, your habits to tick,
  and the month’s money. Each card opens the place it comes from. (#108)
- **To do:** every open to-do in one list, by board, with a line at the bottom to add one. ⌥Return in the bar makes
  a to-do too. (#108)
- **One bar on top of every screen** to write it down, find it or ask (⌘K): Return saves a sticky to Just in, ⌘Return
  asks. (#108)
- **Ask beside whatever you’re doing:** the Ask button opens it as a panel on the right; Esc puts it away. (#108)
- **Plain words:** topics and the canvas are boards everywhere now. Appearance is Light, Dark or Auto (the backdrop
  and blur were for the desk). (#108)
- Fixes: a board opened from Coming next shows as a Board from its first moment, and only one Undo line shows at a
  time. (#108)
- **Meetings make to-dos:** each thing promised in a meeting OSAT wrote up is a to-do of its own in Just in, linked
  to the write-up (Undo takes them back with it). Someone else’s reads as waiting on them: “Sam: send the price
  list”. (#109)
- **To-dos from Granola:** once Granola is connected, OSAT looks once an hour for new meetings and brings each
  one’s Next steps into Just in as to-dos, with a link back to the meeting. Meetings has the switch, Look now and
  Take them back. Nothing is written to Granola. (#109)
- Rooms keep their own margins inside the window again, and the on/off switch looks the same everywhere. (#109)
- **Goals** (Life → Goals, and a card on Today): the few big things, each with how far along it is. A goal can move
  with a board (its to-dos done) or a habit (its days kept this week), so it moves when you do; or tick it off
  yourself. Mark done and Delete have Undo. (#110)
- **Polish:** moving between rooms, each one fades in gently (unless Reduce motion is on). The welcome says what
  OSAT does for you first. Cards say where a sticky came from in today’s words (no more Desk or Sky), and a picture
  in a sticky reads as [Screenshot]. (#111)

## Build 299 · October 16, 2026 — Connections, Soul and memory, Granola sign-in

  calendar: On its own or Ask me). Ask reads the first two before every answer; filing reads what it knows. (#106)

## Build 285 · October 15, 2026 — Boards, a kind for every sticky, Habits, pictures

- **The canvas is boards.** Your boards (OSAT, Fortis, Home…) down the side with Just in on top. Each board shows as
  a Map (a column per kind), a Board (Later, Next, Doing, Done) or a List. Write at the bottom and it lands in the
  right column; drag a sticky to another column, stage or board; right-click to rename or delete a board (its
  stickies go back to Just in). Every change has Undo. The old mind map and its sorting screens are gone. (#102)
- **Every sticky says what it is:** Idea, Question, Problem, To-do, Date or To buy, worked out right away and offline,
  with the AI deciding the unclear ones. Notes keeps Questions, To buy and Coming up for you. A date goes on your
  calendar by itself, with Undo. Just in replaces Unsorted. (#98)
- **Habits, redesigned:** tap to keep a habit today, "9 days in a row · Best: 20", a week strip to fix a past day, a
  month of history, and one-tap ideas to start. Never scolds. (#101)
- **Every AI model downloads whole,** its picture part included, and the AI on this Mac reads pictures: paste, drop
  or attach one in Ask. Settings → AI says what each model can do (pictures, thinking, code). (#100)
- **Mail** is fast on a big inbox, never pops the Mail app open, and has one button to add Gmail. (#99)
- The plan for the rework: write it down, OSAT puts it where it goes (Phases 67–71). (#97)
