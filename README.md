# OSAT Field (copy)

A copy of the OSAT Field prototype, redesigned on September 23. The original OSAT Field folder, OSAT V2, and the installed apps are untouched, and this copy keeps its own browser data.

**The Mac app:** `/Applications/OSAT Field.app`. After changing the code, double-click **Update OSAT Field.command** to rebuild and reinstall it; your notes stay in `~/Library/Application Support/OSAT Field`. A DMG to share is in `release/`.

**The browser preview:** double-click **Open OSAT Field.command** for http://127.0.0.1:5239/. It keeps its own separate data.

Home is a quiet desktop over a blurred wallpaper. Your day and the next steps sit in glass cards on the left. One line sits in the middle: choose Note, Next step, Ask or Search, and Return does that one thing. Your pinned notes, folders and recent notes sit on the right as icons; click one to open it as a sheet. The dock at the bottom goes to every room, and its More menu switches between the lake and the moss wallpapers. Type anywhere on home and the words land in the line.

Inside a room, the bar across the top has Notes, Mindmap, Journal, Calendar and Local AI (⌘1–⌘6). On a phone, the bar moves to the bottom.

An empty home shows a sample room first. Nothing in that sample is saved until you choose **Keep this room**.

The Mac app adds a real browser (⌘7) with **Clip to OSAT**, which saves the selection or the page as a note, and a terminal (⌘8) running your own shell. Every room is in the menu bar with ⌘1–⌘8.
